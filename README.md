A simple, beginner-friendly guide to everything you need to know before watching **Avengers: Doomsday** in theaters on **December 18, 2026** — without having to watch 28 years of Marvel movies and shows first.

## Who This Is For
- Anyone planning to watch *Avengers: Doomsday* with friends, family, or a partner who hasn't kept up with every Marvel movie.
- Longtime fans who want a quick refresher on the characters, timelines, and post-credits scenes leading into opening night.

- **Live Countdown & Doomsday Overview** — A live timer to December 18, 2026, an introduction to Doctor Doom, and how the converging worlds fit together.

- **Invite a Friend** — Send the guide straight to whoever you're watching *Doomsday* with via Email, Messenger, X, TikTok, or Instagram.
